How It Works
01 / ENCODER
Feature Extraction

VGG extracts important features from the content and style images.

02 / ADAIN
Style Transfer

AdaIN aligns style statistics with content features. Alpha controls the strength of the effect.

03 / DECODER
Image Reconstruction

The decoder creates the final stylized image, which Flask returns to the user.
